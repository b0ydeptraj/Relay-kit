# scope-discipline operator contract

Use this utility only when a proposed change may add complexity beyond the stated contract.

## Required evidence

- acceptance criteria and current implementation surface
- each proposed abstraction, dependency, loop, or orchestration step
- a minimum complete contract and a stopping rule

## Verdicts

Classify every addition as `required`, `justified`, `deferred`, or `removed`. Preserve safety, correctness, accessibility, and compliance controls even when they cost tokens or code.
