Relay-kit · Design System v1.0
relay·kit

One system, every surface.

A single tokens.json drives web, desktop (Qt & ImGui) and mobile (Flutter & React Native). Change one value, re-run build_tokens.py, and the whole identity moves together. Accent shown: emerald — swap the primary family to re-theme all platforms at once.
